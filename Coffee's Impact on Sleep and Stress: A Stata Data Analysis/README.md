# ☕ Coffee's Impact on Sleep and Stress: A Stata Data Analysis

Welcome to the project repository for the statistical analysis of coffee consumption and its effects on sleep patterns and stress levels.

## 📝 Project Overview
**Final research project for the Basic Statistics (RCC4705) course at the Faculty of Economics, Administration, and Accounting of Ribeirão Preto (USP).**

This study provides a comprehensive quantitative analysis of how daily coffee consumption impacts sleep duration and stress levels, utilizing hypothesis testing and multiple regression on the Global Coffee Health Dataset.

## 📂 Repository Contents
Here is a breakdown of the files included in this project:

- **`Global Coffee Health Dataset.xlsx`**: Dataset from kaggle.com
- **`Analisi dei dati com Stata - L'impatto del Caffè sul sonno e sullo stress.dta`**: The main Stata dataset containing the variables, observations, and raw data used for the analysis.
- **`Research.pdf`**: The comprehensive final report detailing the research methodology, statistical models applied, and the conclusions drawn from the data.
